Let me ask you this one question. Why do people follow self-improvement these days? Like why do they even click on every motivational video and all? How are these motivational speakers or shall I say motivational businessmen making profits off young people without giving the guarantee of healing from their pain or even guiding them slightly?

Yes, this comes from my personal experience. I was once a motivation addict. I used to save all motivational screenshots, and videos around 7 years ago.

And yes just like everyone it didn't do me any good. But I loved the dopamine, adrenaline rush that I got.

The only thing you won't find in other living creatures is a fully functional human-like brain.

By default, our brain craves something that can hook us for a long time (a very long time). We subconsciously crave routine and habits. We don't usually work hard to form a healthy routine or habits unless we are forced to do so or don't have any other option.

Can be an outside force (another human, situation, etc) that forces us to follow that routine. But we also form bad habits and routines without even working a little hard, we don't even know we are addicts, hooked on bad habits and all unless we try to walk in the opposite direction.

If we don't assign ourselves a job that we would love to do till our last breath then we will be assigned one by the society. (by society I mean the humans, the world we live in. Everything around us plays a role in deciding the direction of our life)

The rare ones who have decided their own life usually didn't do it with the help of external motivational stuff. They found something inside themselves.

- A purpose
- A reason
- A not so smooth but functional way to walk ahead forgiving their past self.

Why do you think you need self-improvement when a well-dressed YouTuber tells you what to do in your life without knowing anything about you?

We think that guy knows us better than ourselves or probably is more experienced in life. Or maybe we can relate to every issue they pretend to address.

Or we are just hooked onto the dopamine shots by his MOTIVATIONAL speech (you'll get rich, have a great life, and so on).

They make courses on it, you buy them. Then you comment there (thanks sir, it really helped me, you're great). Wait, you were successful just after watching that course? Didn't you use that course to gain that particular skill or apply those principles in your life and observe the results, then should have commented. Shouldn't you? (maybe this sounds cringe, forget it)

How can someone else tell us, what's wrong with us?

1. Maybe they can relate with us.
2. Maybe they are just using psychological tricks on us.
3. Maybe they have found the secret to live life.
4. They are enlightened?
5. I really don't know.

But what I now know is the solution we seek in others for the problems that our own mind has created lies within us. But yes some people really can help us with some amazing strategies to cope with our mind. I am aware of many people who are conscious of their mistakes and are doing what they can to do good in life, just not pretending to others and lying to themselves.

We know when we are hungry, when we are thirsty, when we want to hug or vent our emotions. But usually we don't know the right way to do it. ( just a metaphor)

We eat junk and bad food (for instant gratification that we learned from others), and bottle up our emotions until we burst into tears. Another sad night.

Not the right way always leads to not so good consequences.

In simple terms,

You want to live a life that you want to, get rid of your problems, want to have something or want to leave something, etc. But how?

- Have you tried journaling (ever tried to notice a pattern in your thoughts, problems, quality of life by writing them down on PAPER?)
- That’s it for now. Maybe just try it.
- Yes, this newsletter as of now is not a self-improvement letter, I am just asking you all some questions.

In my life experience, I learned, that we suffer until we have learned the lesson life wants us to learn. The definition of success varies from person to person. But the common thing about that definition is "I want to live, not just to be alive".

What about me?

I walked a very less walked path because it was unique for me as I was born 23 years ago but became conscious just about a few years ago slowly very slowly. I am not the person whose advice you should take regarding anything unless you agree with me because I'm still learning about my conscious self. I'm not even slightly awakened yet.

I had this habit of questioning everything since my childhood. Knowledge is my curse as well as a blessing. (You'd know this if you're around me)

I came to my senses when I realized we think we need fixing because we think something is not right inside us or is broken. But now I think, we don't need any fixing, there's nothing inside us that needs the fixing. Every damaged (not broken) piece inside us tells a unique story about us and that damage was already done. There's nothing you can do to change it but can always learn from it. It heals when you realize, the damaging moment is in the past now, left us. One day we will also leave. The damage that was done to us will cease to exist and will become nonexistent because no one will be there to talk about it after a while. Why do we talk about it with ourselves now? We may think it shouldn't have happened to us. But maybe it should have.

It changed something inside of us and that change will change our course of direction in life.

All this is just a random thought that I wanted to share. Nothing else.

If you can associate with any of this, let’s connect and have a chat about life as friends. I am also learning. Comment below your thoughts or you can reply to this email as well.

Have a nice day.
