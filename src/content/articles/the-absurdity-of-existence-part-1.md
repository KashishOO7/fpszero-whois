### The Absurdity of Existence — Part 1

> Why only exist? When you can live.

I’m sharing one of my eBooks here in parts. I hope you all enjoy reading it.

### **INTRODUCTION:-**

Sometimes the little things aren’t the little things. They are the mightiest things. Life is tragically beautiful but we often forget to stop and observe the beauty of it. We worry in sad times and hurry in happy times so we might not miss the moments. And that’s when we miss every inch of it. My first writing wasn’t the happy one but I’m glad I started writing.

It helped me and I guess it must have helped someone else as well. I’m not good at introductions. So, I will keep it short. I’m writing this because I wanted to share my thoughts and emotions creatively and this is the best I have got at the moment. Thanks for taking the time and reading my piece, it means the world to me. If you have anything to share, please reach out to me or if you have any opinion.

### LONELINESS IS MY FRIEND

What does it feel like to be lonely? Does it hurt somewhere in your heart when you realize that no one is waiting for you or missing you? You’re on your own. We were born alone and we die alone but in between, there’s a journey that we are a part of. And in that we need support and, on some days, we have to become the support. There are days when I’m sad and lonely. I feel like staying inside covered in a blanket but that seems like a depressing ending to the day. Do you know what’s scary here? Realizing that sometimes no one can help you. No one. You’re the only best chance you’ve got at this stage of life. Lose it or use it. It’s your call. I don’t know why I have experienced some things that I shouldn’t have or maybe I was very young for that and I still am.

We’re all passengers in this world and time is our transport, we all board in the morning and de-board at night. That’s why we say, the journey is more important than the destination. Some of us have some directions or destinations but some of us don’t even have a sense of awareness. There are days when I ask why do I need to wake up tomorrow? What’s left for me anyway. What’s my purpose anymore. I’m not weak but I’m not strong enough as well. We are not lonely when we don’t have anyone to talk to or no friends but we are lonely when we don’t have any hope left for tomorrow or even today. That’s the moment when we lose our senses, direction, and our mind.

With humanly perspective, you should know life is unfair, nothing always happens for a reason. They just don’t want you to lose hope that’s why they tell you everything happens for a reason and God has a bigger plan for you. The thing they don’t tell you is existence creates all possibilities. Everything is connected with each other. Whenever you’re full of happiness, do you ever cuss at anyone and say that my life sucks, I hate this life, why I’m so unlucky. I guess NO! It’s just we all are a hypocrite. We have different faces to show everyone. God does have a plan for you and it all started just after the second you came into existence.

You will be happy today because someone you met just now appreciated so much about you because they were themselves happy today, most probably something good happen to them today that’s why they wanted to share it with others and make their day bright. Everything is connected and to find the meaning of your life is the meaning of your life. Your life was meant to be yours. So, claim it. Don’t waste it by letting someone else control it for you. Life doesn’t work on autopilot or co-pilot, you’re the one and only pilot here. Take all the responsibilities into your hand, don’t hide and run from them. You never know, running from it might hurt you even more in the ways you never thought.

And if we talk about karma, I think it’s all just about consequences. If you do something in a particular way then something else will happen because of that as everything has its consequences. Break someone’s trust and you will lose them forever, it’s not like karma will punish you for breaking their trust. They are leaving you because of what you did to them and that’s a consequence of your actions and words.

### IS LIFE REALLY TOO SHORT?

When we die, we cease to exist. You can die for someone and can still live for others somewhere else. Life is not only about suffering but it’s also about experiencing everything that’s on our plate. You don’t have to die physically to be dead. You can also be dead if you no longer appreciate the breeze through your window and you no longer have any hope left for any good that can happen and be yours. In my life, I have been to places that I shouldn’t have been to, not physically but mentally and emotionally. I have seen what I shouldn’t have.

> Life is dark when you can’t feel the warmth of the sunlight during the day when you resist every bit of the light even at the night. You let the dark swallow you whole. And you are no longer left with any goal.

I wondered what that experience would be like and dived deep inside my mind. And what I find was unsolicited truth that hurts, when we realize that we have become what we always hated, we have gotten to a point in life where even asking for little help seems a pity.

Your life is a story that will flash before your eyes before you die. Or if we go a little back, you’ll get a chance to recite this short story for someone else. Or maybe you’ll re-think something and that will remind you of everything once and for all whenever you’re sad or depressed. You’ll see how far you’ve come and still, you’re nowhere close to where you always wanted to be. This life wasn’t your choice, it was your parent’s. But you have a choice to live or not today that will become a past in your next tomorrow (Your future).

_I asked myself what do people stay alive for?_

I don’t know maybe some kind of happiness or happiness of others. But for me it’s tomorrow, I let myself pass through another dark night again. I wish for tomorrow to bring me more power and courage to fight my inner demons. The ones who keep me awake late at night and make me sleep late in the morning and held me like a prisoner in my mind. I want to fight again because this is not my end. I’ve risen again from my dark depressing past that had become my present for a very long time.

> We, humans, are just some conscious beings living in a body controlled by a highly intelligent brain. We are nothing but a vision. You dream of a person who will love you wholeheartedly, it’s a vision you have of them. We are all part of a story, maybe you’re not in mine and I’m not in yours yet.

> But don’t forget life has no script. Nothing happens for a reason but it gives us a reason to call it a reason. Some are happy and some are sad, maybe right now you’re not but we are all mad. We suffer and call it destiny; we get happiness and call it a fortune. But we still don’t know how exactly the universe works. We are just here to witness the only mere existence. We tell others to live in the present when we carrying the baggage of our past for so long.

Life is manageable, everything is manageable except the fact we are going to die not sooner but later, and don’t worry about the regrets you’re going to carry all the way to your end. Regrets are fun when you realize you did everything you could at that moment which you’re regretting right now. You tell yourself you could have done better but you forget that you already did your best with the mindset you had at that time. Today can be better than yesterday and tomorrow can be greater than today.

You know life is like a shopping mall, you wake up every day to do something, to take something, to go somewhere, to think about something but if you don’t have a to-do list like a shopping list then you’re missing out on so much.

### THEN HOW SHOULD WE REALLY LIVE?

If you ask me how to live a life! I’ll say don’t look back with regret and don’t look forward with expectations. Just plan your next 24 hours as it’s the only best chance you’ve got. Dreams are visions, they can’t always be planned. And plans doesn’t always work like we planned it to be still we can plan. They can only be assumed and wished for.

> Happiness is just a state of mind that can be achieved by anything that releases dopamine in your brain. It’s a neurochemical that happens to be in your brain all the time and it gives you a shot of happiness whenever you do something exciting or pleasurable.

Rules for a happy lifestyle: -

**1) Make reading your habit.**(Read different books. When you read, your brain focuses all your attention on the words you read. You should know that there is nothing as multitasking when it comes to our brain. Our cute brain switches from one task to another and its speed differs with everyone. It almost seems to be multitasking when you’re really good at it. When you read the words, they make a sound in your brain, you hear them inside your brain then you take a picture of that phrase. It all happens very fast. It requires a lot of attention for that task only.)

**2) Maintain a journal.** (Keep track of everything you do in the day before going to bed. Ask yourself “are you satisfied with your performance today”? Any questions that make you think of your actions.) You can do whatever you practice every day, whatever you tell your mind again and again. It’s all about the method and practices.

**3) A healthy diet.**(It’s an old saying “A healthy body can’t live with an unhealthy mind and vice versa”) health should be your first priority, be it your brain or mind. Whatever you eat, exercise is a must, and a healthy sleep schedule is needed. (Don’t eat junk or packed food too much)

**4) Declutter everything around you.**Remove the things you don’t need. And don’t forget to declutter your mind. Remove the unnecessary thoughts and practice meditation.

**5) Your life will completely change when you realize that a human’s greatest asset and treasure is “Time”.** It’s the most precious discovery a human has ever did. (or more like we realized what time is and how we can use it) It can be used as a weapon in need. Not everyone is rich in time but till the day you live, you have all the time in the world.

> We never know what we are going to leave behind. We just run towards something that we can have forever but we always forget there’s no forever. There’s this never-ending cycle of birth and death that happens to be our only destiny. You can be anyone between it but again we always try to be someone else, someone we think would make a better version of ourselves but we end up getting on the wrong side. The wrong we are also we and the right we are also we. It’s up to you to choose the one who can help you be the best you.

Reality is often what your mind sees. If you can train your mind to see the good in everything then reality won’t be that bad either.

I know you still feel like there’s nothing better for you out there and you want to give up. And I won’t say what if there is better.

But I’d say bet everything on tomorrow and don’t give up on today. Because even if you lose, you’re not losing anything but if you gave up, you’re losing your only chance of trying and meeting someone who’s waiting for you.

You are not God! You are just a human, you don’t have to do everything, you don’t have to be everywhere. Just do what you can and make this world slightly a better place to live. Give love and you’ll receive love.

The biggest need of humans is acceptance. We want society and everyone around us to accept us. Pretty sure that’s why we tend to do everything in a so-called normal way that everyone else does.

We try to fit in the society anyhow even if it costs our uniqueness to cease to exist. We choose the path built by others because we are too scared to create our own. I say if you want freedom and happiness then don’t follow the same usual path that everyone follows. Create your own, and find a unique way to do everything. Not everything needs to be done purposefully. Sometimes be random, do random good things to yourself and others. It will give you a slight feeling of control over yourself. And who doesn’t like self-control?
