### The last lost soul on earth!

*Photo by Lukas Rychvalsky*

Imagine you wake up one morning in your room. You lazily glance over at your alarm clock. Panic sets in as you Utter in a low voice, “Oh no, I’m late for college again!”. You rush around, trying to get ready in record time. But as you rush through your morning routine, something feels off. It’s oddly quiet today — no mom yelling or breakfast smell today. You look out of the window and you see not a single soul is walking or any dog barking.

Your heart races as confusion takes over. It’s like you’ve stepped into an alternate reality. You cautiously explore every corner of your home, but it’s deserted. The unsettling thought creeps in, “Why am I the only one here?”

> Suddenly a booming announcement from miles away shatters the stillness. “This is pod 893 leaving for homestead 2, our new earth in T minus 11 days. Please arrive at the base 11 hours before the departure.” You stand there so confused. Stepping outside, you see pamphlets everywhere boldly proclaiming that earth won’t be suitable for life in the next 10 years and humanity must relocate. You can’t help but wonder, “Is this for real?” Deep down, beneath the confusion, excitement bubbles up within you. You decide to prepare for the journey but before that you want to spend a few days in solitude wandering this peaceful earth.

As you walk along, you pull out your phone, feeling the need to document this moment in time. With a mix of emotions, you begin recording a time capsule message, capturing the essence of this surreal experience.

_Here it goes_:

Once upon a time, in the vast expanding universe, there was a luminous blue rock of life, casually known as Earth. On this rock, you’d find many species but the one who thought was superior coined themselves as “The humans”. They had this incredible gift of feeling and experiencing life in a way that no one else could. They were basically living art and poems but they often seemed to underappreciate their own masterpiece.

In their physical forms, humans journeyed through existence, each with their own unique vision. Some dreamt of love, a vision engraved into their **hearts**that felt like an **art**. They were all part of a **story**, even if they didn’t always know what’s the need to **hurry**. Every human played a character in each other’s stories, some stayed for too **long**and some were just a stepping stone waiting for to be **thrown**.

They all were like stars in the sky, some shined the brightest while some diminished a bit sooner. The universe didn’t have a **script**, but they found their own reasons to **predict**. Maybe, the script written by the universe was not much readable by every **human**but some pretended to be **superhuman**. That’s why some tried to escape the **reality**by lying to themselves without a sense of **clarity**.

Some found happiness and called it luck, while others faced challenges and said it was **fate**, not always but founded it out **late**. The universe’s mysteries always haunted them, but they cherished the moments they had. They preached about living in the **present**but sometimes none of them could let go of their past **accidents**. Some were happy and some were **sad**, not always but all seemed to be **mad**. Why? Don’t know the exact reason but it was probably related to the quality of their decisions they **had**.

Life, they believed, was **manageable**, except for one thing — the inevitable end that wasn’t **shareable**. Regrets were odd companions, born from working too hard to not enough. Not everyone got what they wanted, and not everyone got what they needed.

Sometimes, when they were **alone**, they wondered why no one was **home**. Maybe they hadn’t appreciated the moments when company was abundant. But they always tried to remind themselves to appreciate what they **had**, knowing it might not last forever and eventually again they’ll get **sad**.

They remembered that they were once loved by **someone**, that someone had made their life a happy **one**. But sometimes, it all ended because of **misunderstandings**, maybe they were too **demanding**. A common human experience that they **had**. I’m sorry but it was **sad**.

What did they all need?

Was it to feel alive, to feel loved, or to feel cared **for**? Maybe, but still the wanting never **stopped**.

Years passed, their bodies turned **old**but still some loved each other like they were **gold**, and a beautiful **soul**.

Some cried not because they were **old**but because years ago, they had their soul already **sold**.

Some finally understood that life’s absurdity was only a human perspective. To see the bigger picture was to unlock a new **reality**that could unravel the lost **reality**.

They had all experienced things beyond **words**, trying to share them but often struggling with **words**. People usually believed what they could see, hear, or touch, but the world of the unimaginable remained a mystery not heard by all **others**.

In the end, they all shared a common thread — the human experience. It was a journey of ups and downs, of love and loss, of regrets and hopes. And as they continued on their unique paths, they found solace in the fact that they were never truly alone in their quest to understand the beautiful and mysterious happening of life.

Finally, you reached the base and took a breath of relief after seeing a human, a familiar human face. Now you were not alone. You boarded the pod and went to hibernate.

_The end!_
