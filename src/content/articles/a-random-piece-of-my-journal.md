Are you happy today or are you satisfied today after all you have done so far in your life? Maybe you are but most probably you aren’t just like I am not. People around us and in our society say that we should follow them or abide by the rules made by them but in our entire history not a single person has achieved greatness or experienced the treasure of happiness by following them and guess what we are still struggling with this.

The world is getting closer to doomsday and we all know how! Every minute that passes by leads us closer to our end but that’s not what we should think about because it’s not a curse but mortality is a blessing to humankind. Imagine being stuck in this body and in this world forever (the pain, sorrow, tiredness of living and suffering), do you think anyone will be happy?

We can be anything we want as long as we believe in our existence (You and I were destined and meant to be here). We are put on this earth to play a role in someone’s life who will play a role in our life and the life cycle goes on. The stranger whom you saw yesterday that smiled at you or looked sad, might become the most important person in your life someday Or your best friend of 5 years with whom you spent your best moments might become a stranger to you someday.

You will wonder about everything you did for the past 22 years and suddenly pledge to change everything. Or someone you barely knew could leave a great impact on your sense of belief, everything is good as long as we believe in it.

There is so much left to explore before the end begins. There is so much to live for, to love, to fight for. Earth is the smallest unit one could imagine and humankind has so much left to explore and create. The good thing is, we will achieve this but maybe not as how we imagined it to.

Life happens to everyone but not everybody lives it.

I have so many stories to tell and share but I just don't have the right people to share it with, maybe someday I will.

I am experimenting with everything and this is not how I planned to write on this newsletter. So, bear with me. I am working on it. I am not always going to write random things. I will do better. If you have come this far,

Thank you for reading.
