The reason for being anonymous online and hiding your identity is not something because government agents are trying to trace your every online activity but rather to hide from advertisers, bad actors, and other companies who use your data to bombard you with ads and sell your personal data. Also, to stay safe online, you should realize that you don’t know the person who’s really being nice to you online is actually nice or is just a predator or a scammer. So, stay alert and aware of what you talk with them or anyone.

I’ll be talking about everything you need to know to stay safe online and all the not-so-average ways to stay anonymous online. I’ll try to keep this as short as possible.
There’s tracking where you allow the company or website to track you by you consciously giving away your info freely as when you accept the cookies and agree to the terms and conditions without reading them. Google pretty much knows everything about you. The bookmarks you saved in the browser, whatever you do in the browser where your google account is logged in, are saved by Google. You can check and download all your info from your google account, just type “google takeout” in the search bar and follow the steps to download the zip file of your data.

Follow the steps below to upgrade your privacy and maintain your anonymity:-

1. _The world of passwords_: Always create a strong password that would be hard even for you to remember. Yeah, I’m not joking. If you yourself don’t remember your passwords then, no one can guess them or it would be hard even to brute-force them. For this always use a password manager and the inbuilt feature to generate random strong passwords. You only need to remember the master password that should be strong enough and yes it’s possible to remember a single strong password than to recall dozens of weak passwords. If you think why should you use a password manager or any other doubts you have related to it then, refer to this article [here](https://medium.com/@kashishcharaya/how-a-password-manager-works-and-lets-create-one-in-python-language-2fd6d1bd6955). Don’t save your credentials in your browser ever.
2. _The role of IP addresses:_Just remember that you need to hide your IP address from scammers, advertisers, and bad actors (Hackers) but you can’t hide it from government agencies. They will track you down if they want to. If you perform bad or illegal activities, you’ll surely get caught. It’s just a matter of time. 
An IP address is something that makes your device unique and identifiable on the internet. Anyone who has your IP can find your approx location using open source tools and even can use [OSINT](https://youtu.be/gSXBa5RehYk) to track you down. 
Always use a VPN while browsing the internet using public WIFI. Disable auto-connect to open WIFI networks in settings. Don’t click on suspicious links or attachments in a mail or anywhere on internet, they might be a [IP grabber](https://www.makeuseof.com/ways-ip-grabber-links-security-privacy-risks/) or [BeEF hook.](https://medium.com/@secureica/hooking-victims-to-browser-exploitation-framework-beef-using-reflected-and-stored-xss-859266c5a00a) Wanna know more? Click on the keyword.
3. _Info you share online:_ Don’t add a location near your home on your social media posts. They can lead back to your actual address. Be careful how much info you put online on your social media. Disable app permissions that you don’t use because apps that you don’t use still have the permissions you granted after installing them and can track a lot about you in real-time.

Smart devices like Alexa or others always listen to you. They know a lot about you. I’d suggest staying away from them, you don’t need them. They don’t make your life easier at all, they are just another tracking device.

*image via pexels*

Use Firefox instead of Google Chrome, and always install ad blocker plugins with the highest rating and which are regularly checked for security patches. Turn on auto-updates on all your devices if you usually use unmetered connections like WIFI or check regularly for updates. Updates help you fix vulnerabilities in your system and improve your security.
Be aware of social engineering. Stay alert and aware of what could go wrong with your personal data. Here’s a video regarding “[How to prevent social engineering](https://youtu.be/bU_ydNRZ_9s)”. Keep a single G-mail account for YouTube, Play store and stuff, and Try Proton mail for your email purposes.

Never share your phone with a stranger or leave it without a password. If someone told you that they lost their phone or are unable to call someone and they want your phone to make a call then just politely ask them for the number and dial it for them and after the call delete the call logs in front of them as they might also ask you to delete. But again, we don’t know whom they are calling. So, always inspect a little before helping anyone. Good innocent people want our help but bad ones will disguise their bad intentions under fake generosity.

Everything I talked about in this article is just a basic level of privacy and everyone should follow this. There are so many levels of privacy and anonymity that are not meant for everyone. It requires proper hard and smart work and knowledge. There are lots of other practices that I could have mentioned here but again those are not beginner friendly.

**_Conclusion_**_:- The more private you get online the less personalized recommendations you’ll get from sites, that might also affect your search results on Google. Try searching your full name on Google and see what is visible to everybody, try different filters based on location, education background, career, etc._
