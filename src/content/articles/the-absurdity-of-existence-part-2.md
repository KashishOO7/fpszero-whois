### The Absurdity of Existence — Part 2

If you’re new here, you can read the “The Absurdity of Existence — Part 1” [here](https://medium.com/@charayakashish/the-absurdity-of-existence-49e91afd7f6).

### WHAT IF I DISAPPEARED TOMORROW!

If I was there today that doesn’t mean I’ll be there tomorrow. I might not even exist. And this is a bitter but ultimate truth that no one considers. People do everything as if they have forever to make it happen or fix it but that’s not the case. You can easily plan for something today and tomorrow the reason for that planning might not even exist. So, here we are again at the beginning of the argument. You have all the time in the world as long as you are living in the present but the moment you try to go back to the past, you’ll lose your only reserved time of the present.

> Will anyone miss me if I disappeared tomorrow?

Existence is the ultimate reason for and for everything. People say be nice to everyone but what they don’t tell you is being always nice can harm us in certain ways that you wouldn’t have even considered. There is a quote by George Carlin “**The planet is fine. The people are fuc*ed**”. And that’s true, not everyone is nice around you and a single spoiled person can spoil so many other minds. People can and will disguise their evilness by being nice to you.

I don’t have an answer for every question but I’d say use all your senses all the time, be alert everywhere. It’s our habit to think too much and feel too little. I still believe there are good people out there, you just have to find them and some of them will find you sooner or later but eventually, they will.

_People don’t like my weirdness. They don’t like the messy me_. They don’t even know me. But should I care for all this? The short answer is no. The earth will not stop spinning if I disappeared tomorrow. Nothing will change except the fact that people who love me will miss me. The urge to delete all social media and disappear from everywhere gets overwhelming some days. And we still do that but here we are back again.

### CAN YOU REALLY LOVE SOMEONE WITHOUT LOVING YOURSELF?

They say if you don’t love yourself then no one ever will. If you can’t love yourself then no one else can but hey listen to me I’ve been there. Somedays you’re at your lowest that you can’t even tolerate your regular thoughts. Everything seems so frustrating and outdated. Somedays you can’t help but hate yourself. You watch everything like it’s a movie running in front of your eyes, but you can’t take action. The sad part is you’ve also been there if not then you will be someday because that’s how life works, that’s how you learn and grow.

I sometimes feel like a waste of space maybe everyone felt the same at least once when they were at their lowest. I’ve been lonely for years and now not alone but lonely inside and out.

I spent so many years or I should say wasted so many years looking for that one friend with whom I can have my own time and peace but in that, I lost so much of my time and energy and still haven’t found them. I am on good terms with everyone in my college but I don’t have a connection with them.

I’m so lonely right now that I don’t even know who I am anymore. I’ve lost my sense of presence and am stuck here with your absence. I don’t know you but I feel like I need you now. I don’t know if you exist or not but I need you now. I’m talking about someone who will be my only one once and for all. I don’t know your name. So, I can’t call you but I need you now.

Accept the fact that you can change your life the way you want it to. I can’t easily connect with people because I don’t like meaningless conversations. Mortality is a blessing and an opportunity as life will always remind you of how limited the time you have. So, you can’t afford to waste any more time. You have to keep moving because stopping means death. If you stop breathing, you’ll die, your heart can’t stop pumping blood. Your organs work 24×7 but you have the privilege of resting every day. You can take breaks for as long as you want but you should not stop.

We always get scared and settle for that one relationship where we both don’t respect each other’s boundaries and even though we know we are not right for each other but we think the perfect person for us might not even exist and end up settling for the wrong one. I’m not an ideal person to give relationship advice but I think I understand them. But again, I’ll wait till the day I myself have experienced all this and then, I’ll speak better on all this.

I want to forget all that. I want to move on somewhere in life just away from all this. Like starting a new life with a new name, a new place around new people but that doesn’t seem to happen because of the habits I’ve built. And on the same page, I get scared to leave the ones I love and care for.

### IGNORE THE UNIGNORABLE

Life is very short if you keep on responding to every hateful message in your day-to-day life. Learn the ultimate super of power of ignorance that is considered to be unnatural at times which can be learned not so easily but effectively in this life form. You can’t shut every disgraceful mouth but what you can do is change the way you look at the person.

They might be the victim of their actions and thoughts but you’re not. You can be anything you want to be but you should choose to be your best version. If it was easy to ignore the harmful noise then there wouldn’t be so many conflicts every day.

A person’s future is truly determined by the consequences of his/her past and that is considered to be his/her present. Arguing with everyone will take you nowhere but surely will suck out your spiritual and mental energy. Choose the people you spend your time with wisely. Keep your circle smaller but happier.

### TIME IS THE ULTIMATE TREASURE

I’m never truly gone from somewhere even if I leave it. My mind is still lurking there in the shadows. I ask myself, is there any other way to fix this, or am I just running out of ways to fix my life. It’s more cowardly to live like this. So, I’ve to find my way out of it. I know living every day in fear is more like dying every day in that every moment.

There are happy endings and there are also bad endings. It’s not what someone desired or wanted. You get something because something else happened just before that. It created a pathway for it to reach you.

Trust me when I say, we all have tried enough because the mindset we had, in the beginning, was not the same as now. We gave our best shot at that moment but now we think we could have done it better and all we do now is regret it.

**Regret**is a very weird feeling. Some regret because they worked too hard, cared too much, loved too much but in the end, it didn’t even matter and some regret because they tried too little, worked less, didn’t love and care enough and in the end, it all mattered but they couldn’t make it any better. Not everyone gets what they want and not everyone gets what they need.

Think about time just as food and oxygen. You won’t survive much longer without food and oxygen. Same with time. You don’t want to run out of time until you reach your destination. What if you have already run out of it and you just don’t know it yet? You will surely realize this not when you will look at your watch and realize it’s too late now but when you will look back in time and see you are nowhere near where you always wanted to be and it’s been days, weeks, or even months.

Sadness is real when you’re sitting in a room with no one else and asking yourself, why is no one home? Maybe we didn’t care much when everyone was with us. We just wasted every moment of it on useless and meaningless things. So, let’s take a moment to appreciate everything we have.

It’s just a matter of time when whatever you have will be long gone maybe forever.

Remember you were loved by me. You made my life a happy one. But it all ended just with a misunderstanding and yes it happens all the time with everyone.

I say it’s not too late to start a completely new life. Age can just affect your body and mind but it can’t break your willpower, your desire to learn and grow, to find a way to live your life. In the end, what do we all need? We just need to feel alive, feel loved, and cared but do we all of us get that or get any of that? The easy answer is, that it depends. Some of us get a little of that but we don’t appreciate that little.

### DOES YOUR PAST STILL EXIST? ARE YOU READY FOR YOUR FUTURE?

It’s not how far you’ve decided to go but it’s how far you have come. Do you think the past is gone, all erased, and the future is just a void yet to be filled? I don’t know, let’s talk about it.

I remember having an exam the next day and I was stressing too much about it and I wondered does it really matter what I’m doing. Tomorrow hasn’t arrived yet and doesn’t know if it will but I have to be fully prepared for tomorrow and that doesn’t mean killing my brain cells with too much unnecessary stress. No one will remember this exam after a few days or weeks but this stress will become a habit of me that will hurt me for years and that’s not something I’d want for myself.

Our future is directly influenced by what we are doing right now in present. So, we should better look at the steps and the direction we are walking in rather than worrying about the destination itself. It’s true that we want to re-live some of our past moments that not for too long. Short-live past happy moments are better than long-live past traumas. But sometimes it’s hard to differentiate between them and we choose the wrong ones to re-live that re-builds the guilt and freshen the wounds. Sometimes you’re too afraid to say goodbye to your past and that’s ok. You just need to re-consider the fact that the past just happened and the future will happen too. It’s all inevitable.

### I BELIEVE IN GOD BUT SOMEDAYS I DON’T!

Unanswered prayers lead to self-questioning. I personally don’t think God is living above us in the sky, watching us every moment and judging us on our actions and thoughts, and sending us to heaven and hell based on it.

God is everywhere and living among us. But somedays it’s hard to trust that when you can’t talk to them or see them but you still have to believe in them. Faith and hope are the key ingredients in the survival of this human life. We don’t get what we want or need, we just get what we get. There’s no one to blame for it. If life was fair and everyone was equal then everyone would be happy, life would be perfect and no-one would miss God or even recognize their presence.

### WHY AM I WRITING THIS?

I’m not an open book, I’ve secrets to hide and I’ve been hiding them for years. Days, weeks, months, and even years have passed but the pain is still in the heart, wounds are fresh and even they are stretched.

The past few years have taught me so much that I couldn’t have learned anywhere. I’d say life was never fair, is not fair, and will never be fair. We are just hypocritical beings living on an isolated planet in this universe. As we are, trying to find other life forms in this universe because we all are lonely beings but trying not to be. Some people have friends and some people don’t but what we all have is hope. We hope someday we will meet someone that will bring peace to us and an end to our loneliness but if that person themselves is lonely and they also looking for us. Then what’s the whole point of hoping?

> I am writing this in a hope of touching your heart. I hope we can be friends. I hope my words meant something to you. And it’s just a life, it happens to everyone, don’t take it seriously. Hundreds of people are born every day and they don’t have what you have.

### SEE YOU ON THE OTHER SIDE OF LIFE!

Pain is everywhere around you. If you’ll look for it, you’ll become the pain. Instead, just create your happiness by balancing your emotions and thoughts. Once someone said to me, you can’t and shouldn’t bottle up your emotions otherwise one day you’ll shatter into pieces just like when the pressure builds up in a closed container and it bursts out eventually. The moments between birth and death are maybe random and caused by some consequences but it’s our habit to blame God and our life. Your life is not a mistake, it’s an opportunity. Claim it. Be the first one to discover your inner strength and abilities that some are considered to be natural and unnatural. Sometimes we should just let things go and let some things in but we choose to linger with them as if it’s meant for us but not really. You can’t control everything but you can control your mind and body. And that’s enough.

I’ve loved someone and had to let them go because I wasn’t their choice. Love can only be felt because it can’t be described by words. It’s just a matter of time before that feeling fades away.

**I have a question for you….**

Where are you right now? No, I’m serious. Look at your surroundings, for how long you have been here? I don’t know but you know for sure. But what I know is, it’s more than enough. If you won’t go out and explore the unexplored then you have no right to complain about living a boring life. And yes, it can be hard or really hard for you to get a change but not impossible. There are people out there who are just like you and they like people just like you. Great people will not always find you, sometimes you have to find them. Good luck on your journey. I wish the journey brings you more joy than destiny itself.

I’ve decided to look at the change the way I want to, not as life wants me to.

Life doesn’t always have to be dark and depressingly deep. You can still find the meaning in your life without getting depressed, not everybody needs rock bottom to wake up to reality. Just don’t stop asking questions to yourself daily. Life is full of moments that make it beautiful. We aren’t just living to die; we all are explorers and travelers. To the world, you may just be another person but to that one person, you are their whole world and that’s enough for you to stay happy forever. I don’t know how to make you believe this but there are millions of people who will love you, there are thousands of people just like you, and all you need is just a single partner to spend your whole life with. But for that, you have to go out and explore. Do what you love and create what you love. And you will meet them in no time.

Life gets easier when you realize that the universe doesn’t care about your feelings. But some people do, don’t lose them.

Elbert Hubbard said, “_Do not take life too seriously. You will never get out of it alive_”. I finally understood this. There will be moments that will break you but that’s not the end of your life. Life will come to an end, and so will the good times, bad times, pleasure, and pain. Do not cling to a feeling or a situation for too long. Learn to let it go and practice resilience.

Thanks for reading. You’re an amazing person and you deserve all the happiness in the world but no one always has all the time in the world. So, it’s better to utilize every single second of yours.
