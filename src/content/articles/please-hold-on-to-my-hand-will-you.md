Time is the real deal breaker. Time can get you everything and anything but the only thing it can't get you is time itself. What has passed, is passed. Money is not a treasure. Learning the way of time is the ultimate true value of life. The day you realize the importance of an hour a day a week in a month and so on is the day you will step on the path of enlightenment.

I once felt like I was somewhere near it but I was wrong. We humans love to make a fool out of us and everyone around us. Lying gives us peace which might be temporary but it's enough to survive. And yeah temporary is temporary. The moment we have to face the truth, the true reality, is the moment we get anxious. Not because it might hurt us in many different ways but because it might expose our past and future mistakes that we made consciously. And then we feel shame and disgust, which again is temporary.

Welcome to human life, because that's all I can experience as I'm also a part of this human life.

There is a movie named “Interstellar”, I don’t know if you have watched it or not but you should definitely watch it. (No spoilers ahead)

We are free to do anything or are we? The concept of life is kinda absurd in many ways. We live to die not live to live but why? We all know that as with every food and medicine, we also are born with an expiration date and we don't embrace that but in fact, we fear that. Why?

The short answer would be "the way our minds are programmed since our childhood".

We are fed a bunch of lies since our childhood.

You know time happens to be your only guardian angel and your only destroyer. Time can be manipulated the way you want it to. See, if you can train your mind and body to work more and faster than your current capacity then you can break the time barrier that gives you the regretful feeling of not living and accomplishing your present goals. Life doesn't guarantee you anything but it creates all the possibilities that can happen today, tomorrow, or could have happened yesterday or never.

The only thing you should always look forward to is testing your limits and proving others wrong when they say "It can't be done". Yeah, competing with others is not healthy as it never ends, and as everyone said. But a little competition won't harm you if done in a controlled manner under the supervision of your wise YOU.

If you ask me why I write all of these and things like these, then probably I wouldn't have any solid answer. I started writing to keep my sanity, and to prevent bottling up of my emotions. Then, came the time when I was running behind validation from others (A big mistake I did and we all do). Skipping to the present, I write what I feel and what I see. Thoughts of winning the race of time excite me even though I know it's not possible but we can for sure do better with our lives if we want to and if we figure out how to.

I don't recommend anything in particular, don't listen to me, don't follow me unless you consciously agree with me multiple times on the things I say.
