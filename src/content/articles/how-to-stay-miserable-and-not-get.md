Step 1: Asking everyone for help, considering everyone's good and best by getting blinded by their hall of fame. Not following a single piece of advice.

Step 2: Wanting and looking for free courses, certificates, and not completing or even starting the courses you already have.

Step 3: Looking for motivation all the time, ignoring or running away from self-discipline and self-control.

Step 4: Not realizing and understanding your pattern of procrastination and triggers that lead you to the path that pulls you away from your good life.

Step 6: Always looking at tutorials, roadmaps, and blogs without practicing anything at all, or if practicing a little, not taking notes.

Step 7: Learning but not implementing or testing yourself.

Step 8: Running away from yourself, your true self.

**The roadmap for every road and destination is built differently for every person. You and I are not the architect, the universe is. Want to win your self-respect? Learn to learn well. Learn to hear your instincts.**

We have normalised “following other people's footsteps” but it rarely helps. What worked for others won't necessarily would work for us.

The world is the same but every human's perception isn't. So are the methods and tools we created in our mind.

I say "_The intellect you look for in others is smaller than the one you always held in your heart. So, kindly look inside and you shall find peace and wisdom_".

I am no different, I also make these mistakes. Better to realize it sooner than later.

In today's world, it has become easy for everyone to become great. Because not everyone is aiming to become great, not even a few. So, there's no crowd at the top. People want attention, fame, and a life of luxury but most of them don't deserve it. But still earn it or should I say "they take it with manipulation".

All the best. You are smarter than you think you are. _True wisdom lies in knowing that you don't know everything._
