I had this thought recently, what if we are constantly shifting between different versions of reality all the time without even noticing? The one we are living in transcends time and space. We talk about the multiverse, but what if, as conscious beings, we are already capable of time travel and jumping between alternate realities?

From choosing something to acting upon it, we change the course of our direction, which inevitably leads to different consequences and results. If you had made a different choice at any point, you wouldn't be here today. Your reality, your world, would have been different.

These different sets of choices could be the pathways to different realities (or maybe even different universes). We are not physically going anywhere; our consciousness shifting into a different timeline the moment we make a choice. This means every possible outcome already exists, and by making a decision, we “tune in” to the reality that aligns with that choice.

If realities already exist and we just step into them, does that mean we aren’t truly creating our reality? Not necessarily.

- As we humans are **3 Dimensional beings**, we can’t perceive all possible timelines at once, only the one we are currently aligned with. (Our brain don’t have the mental capacity)
- To us, **stepping into a new reality feels like creating it**, because we lack awareness of all the alternatives that already exist.

Suppose there are two rooms isolated to each other, carrying individual universes in it. In one, the dawn of civilization has just began.:

Homo sapiens are learning to build fire, hunting for survival and mating with each other And in the room next to it (the universe it carries belongs to us) we are building cities that touch the sky, creating machines that think, and searching the cosmos for answers.

The two rooms exist simultaneously, but their realities are vastly different. The civilizations within them are unaware of each other, just as we are unaware of the countless parallel worlds that might exist beyond our current perception.

Now, **what happens when you open a door between them?** Does one reality overwrite the other, or do they simply merge, coexisting in ways beyond our understanding? Maybe stepping into a new reality isn’t about creating it, _it’s about aligning ourselves with what was always there, waiting for us to perceive it. Accept it_.

_What you want in life is already yours, you just have to ask the universe for it with an open heart. I am also learning how to!_

### What do you think? Does reality change when we step into it, or do we change to fit the reality?

If we were **4D beings**, we might see all timelines simultaneously, just like we see all frames of a film at once when editing a movie.

Yes, **free will still exists, just in a different way**, instead of creating, we are **choosing** from an infinite set of possibilities. Think of it like browsing a cosmic streaming service “_**Cosflix**_”, where every possible version of reality is available, and we pick which "episode" to experience based on our actions and energy. We certainly can’t leapfrog jump between episodes, can’t skip intros and outros, can’t pause the moment to grab some snack and get comfy on the sofa OR can We?!

I think we can.

- **Leapfrog jumping between episodes** → Mood swings, emotional turmoil.
- **Skipping intros, outros** → Avoiding reality, being delusional, numbing ourselves with mental stimulation.
- **Pausing the moment** → Being fully present. Just _being_. Not trying to fit in. Not trying _to be_ something.

Reality is just the projection of our consciousness, shaped by our thoughts and experiences.

In the quantum realm, everything is _energy_ and _vibration_. To become a different version of yourself, you would need to either ask the universe for that energy or emit it yourself. There are infinite versions of us.

- Our **thoughts and emotions emit a frequency**, and that frequency determines the reality we experience.
- Quantum mechanics hints at **superposition**, where multiple states exist simultaneously until observed. Maybe our consciousness is the observer that "locks in" a particular reality.
- **Resonance theory** suggests objects vibrating at the same frequency sync up. Could our desires and thoughts work the same way, **aligning us with a reality that matches our internal state**? We often find ourselves surrounded by bad people and situations when we're stuck in bad habits
- This means we’re not "moving" through time but rather **tuning into different frequencies of existence**, much like changing radio stations.

If this is true, reality isn’t set in stone, it’s fluid, shaped by the energy we put out.

### **The “Alien” Hypothesis: A Different Perspective on Us**

What if “aliens” might just be **humans in parallel realms** who understand this process better. With some possibilities:

- Maybe they **exist outside of linear time**, seeing all realities at once.
- They could have **mastered reality shifting**, traveling across timelines like we walk through rooms. (Reference from interstellar, tesseract)

**This makes the Fermi Paradox (the question of why we haven’t found aliens) more intriguing, maybe they’re here, just not in our specific frequency of existence.**

### If our choices already shift us between realities, **Can we consciously control where we go?**

- **Intentional Shifting:** If thoughts and emotions dictate reality, could techniques like **meditation, visualization, or manifestation** help us shift **consciously**?
- **Manifestation vs. Reality Shifting:** People talk about manifesting things into existence, but what if they’re not attracting something new, just shifting into the version of reality where that thing already exists?

If this theory is true, reality is not something we passively experience, it’s something we **navigate** based on thoughts, energy, and choices.

## **Is Time Just Decay?**

One way to think about time is as a **measure of change. The passing of moments.**

- At a biological level, we see time through **aging, decay of matter and universe.**
- At a quantum level, **particles fluctuate, energy transforms, and the universe evolves**, but does time _cause_ this, or is it just how we perceive change?

**So, is time just the decay of matter?**

**LLM said:** Not exactly. **Decay is a process that happens within time,** but time itself is something deeper, something that governs **motion, change, and perception** rather than just biological degradation.

**Reality shifts are possible as long as we’re alive** makes perfect sense. Right?

- Our **brain** is the control center of our **experience of reality**, if it stops functioning, our personal perception of reality ends.
- Consciousness (the **mind**) is not just the brain but the patterns of thoughts, awareness, and decision-making that emerge from it.
- What powers the brain? **Electricity.** Neurons fire using electrical impulses and chemicals, forming the chain of thoughts and choices that shape our experiences.

**So, could all of this "shifting" be a biological process?**

- Maybe we shift through timelines because our brain is constantly making micro-decisions, leading us to slightly different versions of reality (this aligns with the Many-Worlds Interpretation in quantum physics).
- As long as we are alive, meaning our neurons are firing, our heart is pumping, and our sensory organs are processing data, we are actively participating in reality.
- The moment these stop, we don’t know what happens. Does the “observer” disappear, or does it continue in another form? Maybe we finally flow like pure energy through time and space.
