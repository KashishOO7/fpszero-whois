## Beginnings are hard.

Sometimes the little things aren’t the little things. They are the mightiest things. Life is tragically beautiful but we often forget to stop and observe the beauty of it. We worry in sad times and hurry in happy times so we might not miss the moments. And that's when we miss every inch of it. My first writing wasn’t the happy one but I’m glad I started writing. It helped me and I guess it must have helped someone else as well. I’m not good at introductions. So, I will keep it short.

### Why this, why now?

I started writing 2 years ago when I used to post quotes, poems, and short stories on instagram in my free time. I wasn’t consistent enough at posting so I didn’t grow on insta. I also have written two short eBooks that I will be sharing some content from here.

I am launching this personal space where I will be sharing two newsletter per week, one of them will be focused on philosophy (nothing cliche) and another will be Information security and online privacy.

## Thought of the day:

When you don't feel like waking up or going to sleep. When you don't want to talk or just want to listen to someone's vent. When you don't care about past or future, you just want to stay here in the moment. 
This isn't depression, this isn't sadness, this is peace. 
The peace that you might miss or crave for. 
Best conversations, moments, emotions happen at 4 am. 

Don't be shy and share all the things that's in your mind so that people who are just like you can feel your warm presence and those who aren't can understand your presence.

I am open to all types of suggestions and feedback.
