You're living among humans in a planet ran by humans.

One day you became conscious enough to realise whatever happened to you is lost as in past, whatever happening to you is a present aka a gift you have without asking, and whatever is about to happen that you keep on worrying about is yet to come a future.

Okay. I understand this is time, right?

Nah nah.

This is just an analogy to understand the happening of events.

Imagine you're floating in space and are able to view the blue luminous earth (our dear home) as well.

But you're also present on earth at the same time among your family, friends, lover or maybe strangers with mutual memories. Ok shouldn't have said that.

So, on earth you think a certain way, have a particular thought, perform a particular action. Everything has its consequences.

In space at the same time, every thought you had, action you lead, makes you hold an invisible thread of time.

These threads, oh they are infinite as for an individual's consciousness.

While holding that thread of time, you move ahead in life aka time and experience stuff that came to existence because of YOU my friend.

Different thoughts|action|a little bit pinch of fate I shall say, gets you to hold a thread parallel to your current one.

We travel in life the way of thread of time.

To lose a thread and not hold another one, we lose our consciousness or shall I say life!

While floating in this infinite space, we tend to get lost as we don't know which thread to hold next or which to leave yet.

Yes, it's possible to hold two of em as we get prepared to leave the previous one. But we can't move in life while clinging to two simultaneously.

Sad depressing thoughts, poor or bad habits drags us towards weak and poor quality of threads that can break and leave us hanging forever in space.

Quality of thread depends on our quality of choices. We are always given a choice, it's up to us to choose a thread with better and smooth ride in space. Remember, every thread offers a unique experience.

Just jump to different ones for a vast variety of taste.

Who am I?

I'm a survivalist.

Yet to measure the vastness of this infinite space.

Oh did I say infinite?

It's for you, not for me.

From an individual's perspective it's always limited for oneself.

For the other it's infinite because we are not them. We can't experience their life and consciousness.

Let's take rest now. Bye take care.
