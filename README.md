# whois.fpszero.com

The personal site of Kashish Charaya (FPSZER∅): writer, physicist and cyber security expert.

**Live:** [whois.fpszero.com](https://whois.fpszero.com)

## What is on it

The site is a small desktop. Each window is one part of it:

- **Writing.** Every piece, with folders, subjects, search and sorting.
- **Workshop.** The projects: fpszero.com, Spectra, fpszero-lab, Svalekh and AEGIS.
- **Inbox.** A few short conversations: who I am, what FPSZERO is about, the first piece on A Principia and a thought from Beyond the Absurd. They are written, not live. To reach me, use LinkedIn or email.
- **Reading list.** Save pieces to read later. It lives on your device only.
- **Terminal.** Move around the site with commands: `ls`, `cat` an article, `sites`, `visit`.
- **About** and **Settings.**

Every window has its own address (`#writing/<slug>`, `#about`, `#inbox` and so on). Every piece also has its own plain page, and the home page falls back to a plain page when scripts are off.

## How it behaves

- No cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
- Nothing loads from another domain. The page's content security policy forbids it, and the network icon in the menu bar lists every request the page made.
- What you choose (look, windows, reading list, terminal files) is kept in your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to a server. Settings can clear it.
- Fonts are the ones already on your device, so nothing is downloaded.
